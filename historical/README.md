# Historical Research Archive

The documents contained in this directory represent historical stages of the research program that eventually led to Beating Substrate Theory (BST).

They are preserved for:

- transparency,
- traceability,
- reproducibility,
- and historical continuity.

These documents should not be interpreted as the current formulation of BST.

The consolidated BST framework supersedes the individual formulations presented in these historical papers while preserving their historical role in the development of the reconstruction program.
